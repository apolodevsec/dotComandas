## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the quiet luxury, intimacy, and architectural poise of high gastronomy and boutique hospitality. It speaks to discerning patrons seeking curated culinary journeys, private dining, and effortless reservation experiences. 

The aesthetic is classical contemporary minimalism. It balances deliberate restraint with sensory warmth: tactile dark charcoal surfaces, radiant sand-gold accents, and expansive editorial whitespace. Visual rhythm is established through striking contrast between statuesque serif displays and pristine, functional sans-serif microcopy. The emotional tone must evoke timeless dignity, impeccable hospitality, and quiet confidence—avoiding superficial ornamentation in favor of architectural proportion, bespoke lines, and refined typography.

## Colors

The color palette centers on tactile immersion, using a foundational dark canvas accented by metallic sand-gold and luminous off-white.

- **Primary (`#C49A6C`)**: Warm sand-gold. Used intentionally for focal highlights, active states, delicate keyline borders, curated badges, and high-priority interactive accents. It provides warmth without becoming gaudy brass.
- **Secondary / Surface Light (`#F7F7F7`)**: Pristine warm off-white. Used primarily for high-contrast inverted light cards, menu paper sheets, primary light-theme surfaces, and prominent light typography.
- **Neutral Base (`#2A2A2A`)**: Deep tailored charcoal. Serves as the primary dark backdrop, evoking evening candlelight and stone textures rather than sterile pure pitch-black.
- **Neutral Dark / On-Light Text (`#333333`)**: Deep graphite used exclusively for text and structural iconography when rendered on off-white (`#F7F7F7`) surfaces.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)**: High-contrast display typography, primary titles, and hero messaging over dark charcoal surfaces.

### Semantic Application & Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base**: `#2A2A2A` for default immersive luxury dark mode.
- **Elevated Canvas / Layer 1**: `#333333` for subtle panel lift, sheet overlays, and modal backgrounds.
- **Dividers & Strokes**: `#C49A6C` at 30% to 60% opacity for hairline borders (0.5px to 1px) and structural framing.
- **Muted Body Text**: `#F7F7F7` at 70% opacity on dark backgrounds; `#333333` at 70% opacity on light paper cards.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the editorial grace of `Playfair Display` with the balanced, technical clarity of `Plus Jakarta Sans`.

- **Editorial Headlines**: All major headings and section titles use `Playfair Display`. Titles lean into natural, unhurried leading and light-to-medium weights, evoking Michelin-starred tasting menus and heritage wine lists.
- **Curated Uppercase Details**: `label-caps-*` roles must strictly be rendered in uppercase with generous letter spacing (18% to 25%). These are reserved for service categories, course distinctions (e.g., "ANTIPASTI", "DEGUSTAZIONE"), table metadata, and interactive button triggers.
- **Clarity in Inputs & Data**: Form fields, reservation pickers, currency figures, and culinary descriptions rely on `Plus Jakarta Sans` for effortless legibility under both ambient dining light and digital screens.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout adopts a disciplined editorial grid inspired by classical book design and architectural floor plans. Generous vertical pauses between sections reinforce an unhurried, luxurious cadence.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1024px and above)**: 12-column symmetrical grid with a maximum content container of 1320px. Margins set to `margin-desktop` (`5rem`), with consistent gutters of `2.5rem`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8-column grid with `2rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (below 768px)**: 4-column fluid grid, utilizing `margin-mobile` (`1.25rem`) and `gutter-mobile` (`1rem`).

### Rhythm & Alignment
- **Editorial Asymmetry**: Content columns often alternate with empty 2-column gutters on desktop to let imagery and bespoke menu compositions breathe.
- **Hero & Storytelling Bands**: Spacers between core culinary chapters must use `space-2xl` or `space-3xl` to preserve white space as an active design asset.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects deep drop shadows and synthetic floating blurs, favoring tactile structural framing and subtle tonal layering.

- **Low-Contrast Hairline Outlines**: Depth is created using 1px borders colored in gold (`#C49A6C`) at 25%–40% opacity or graphite (`#333333`) against the `#2A2A2A` background. This mimics metallic inlay work in fine interior joinery.
- **Tonal Stepping**: 
  - Canvas: `#2A2A2A`
  - Elevated Container / Cards: `#2F2F2F` or `#333333`
  - Floating Overlays / Modals: `#232323` framed by a 1px solid `#C49A6C` border.
- **Ambient Glow on Hover**: When an interactive element rises or receives focus, it does not cast an opaque dark shadow; rather, it manifests an ultra-diffused, ambient gold radiance: `0 8px 32px rgba(196, 154, 108, 0.08)`.
- **Inverted Light Sheets**: Menu cards and tasting notes occasionally leverage crisp `#F7F7F7` sheets placed flat atop the dark field, framed with a subtle 1px border of `#C49A6C` (50% opacity) without drop shadows.

## Shapes

The design system strictly adheres to crisp, rectilinear geometry (`roundedness: 0`). 

- **Sharp Corners (0px)**: All buttons, cards, dialogs, inputs, image frames, and badges feature razor-sharp right angles. This architectural geometry conveys precision, European culinary heritage, and bespoke craftsmanship.
- **Dividers & Framing**: Fine decorative borders, bracketed corners, and subtle crosshair indicators may be used at the intersections of cards or section boundaries to evoke bespoke architectural blueprints and classical menu folios.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Sharp rect frame with solid gold background (`#C49A6C`), label rendered in `#2A2A2A` using `label-caps-md` (uppercase, letter-spacing `0.22em`). Hover shifts background to `#F7F7F7` with text `#2A2A2A` via smooth transition (300ms ease).
- **Secondary / Outlined Action**: Transparent background with a 1px border of `#C49A6C`. Label in `#F7F7F7` or `#C49A6C` uppercase text. Hover fills the container with `#C49A6C` at 12% opacity.
- **Text Action**: Unframed button using `label-caps-md` with an underlined hairline indicator in `#C49A6C` positioned 6px below text.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Form Controls**: Architectural underline-only or minimal rectilinear outline style. Background is transparent or `#232323`. 
- **Borders**: 1px bottom border in `#C49A6C` (40% opacity) at rest; transitions to 100% `#C49A6C` with zero shadow on focus.
- **Typography**: Labels set in `label-caps-sm` resting above the input; actual user input text rendered in `#FFFFFF` with `body-md` in `Plus Jakarta Sans`. Placeholder text in `#F7F7F7` at 40% opacity.

### Cards & Menu Items
- **Tasting / Dish Cards**: Framed in 1px `#333333` or `#C49A6C` (25% opacity) over `#2F2F2F` background. High-resolution imagery sits flush to borders without radius.
- **Menu Row Item**: Dish title in `Playfair Display` (`headline-sm`), dotted or hairline continuous rule leading to the price in `#C49A6C` (`Plus Jakarta Sans`), with culinary provenance and ingredients beneath in `body-sm` (`#F7F7F7` at 75% opacity).

### Chips & Badges
- **Status / Dietary Tags**: Sharp rectangular tags. Transparent background with 1px hairline border in `#C49A6C` (60% opacity). Typography uses `label-caps-sm` in `#C49A6C`. Zero border-radius.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Checkboxes**: Precise 16x16px squares with 1px border of `#C49A6C`. Checked state fills the box with `#C49A6C`, showing an inset `#2A2A2A` geometric check icon.
- **Radio Buttons**: 16x16px sharp outer square enclosing a centered solid 6x6px gold square indicator when selected.

### Reservation Date & Time Selector
- **Calendar Grid**: Strict tabular border matrix with hairline `#333333` dividers. Selected dates show solid `#C49A6C` block background with `#2A2A2A` text. Available slots highlighted with gold outline on hover.
